The harness is open source and free to use. If you would rather have it working for your team without spending
the time yourself, V and Bruno LLC, who build and maintain it, can help.

## What we can do

- **Custom setups.** The harness, a model server and your repositories set up on your machines, with the model
  profiles, gates and limits tuned to your hardware and your code.
- **Private stacks.** Astro is the first supported stack. If your team works in another framework or a private
  toolchain, we can add a stack for it: detection, gates and their diagnostic parsers, and the evaluation tasks
  that prove it works.
- **Fine-tunes.** A local model trained on your codebase's conventions and your own past changes, measured with
  the harness's evaluation set before and after, so you can see what it gained.
- **Astro sites.** If what you need is the site itself, we build those too.

## Get in touch

Tell us what you are working with and what you want it to do: [vandbruno.net](https://vandbruno.net/).

For bugs and feature requests in the harness itself, open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/Viktotovich/vb-harness-for-agentic-building/issues).
